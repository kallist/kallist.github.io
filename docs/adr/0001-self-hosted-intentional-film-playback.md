# Self-host films with intentional playback

The portfolio remains a GitHub Pages static export. Three supplied videos are served as bounded web derivatives with posters and no video request until a visitor chooses Play. This retains source control and avoids third-party tracking/embedding dependencies, at the cost of repository size and hosting bandwidth. Large original masters stay outside the repository; attribution and derivation records stay with the assets. No sound or video auto-starts on entry, and changing works stops the previous playback.
