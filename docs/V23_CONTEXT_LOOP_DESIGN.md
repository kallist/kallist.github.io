# V2.3 living ASCII context loop

## Concept and visual boundary

The site's fixed character world becomes one continuous context loop: observe, retrieve, act, remember, trace, evaluate and replay. It is a broad, asymmetrical figure-eight ribbon with a half twist, not a torus or loading indicator. The form is produced by characters on a sampled surface; no line, filled mesh or image sits underneath them. Paper, graphite and warm gray follow the existing editorial rails. The reference supplied by the owner informed the principle of internally moving character imagery; its shape, source, assets and timing are not reused.

## Geometry and glyphs

The centerline is a closed figure-eight with different left and right lobe widths. A local cross-section rotates by half a turn around the centerline, making its front/back relationship change continuously. Perspective projection, depth ordering, transverse width and sparse edge sampling make the band read as a surface. The crossing has distinct front and rear depths. A seeded generator assigns stable symbol families and very rare context words; their density creates the visible band rather than decorating a pre-drawn outline.

## Motion and intro

The shape remains spatially stable while glyph coordinates advance around its closed parameter, with small surface tension shifts, edge-density breathing and rare within-family substitutions. Pointer influence is local and bounded. A faint glyph preview is visible before hydration. Canvas first draws sparse arcs, resolves the twist and closes the loop by roughly 1.2 seconds; circulation becomes apparent afterward. Rails and Hero register after the shape starts to resolve, without a blocking loader.

## Composition, accessibility and performance

One fixed, pointer-transparent Canvas sits above section surfaces and below real content. Per-chapter tone and per-content occlusion zones reduce glyph strength behind text, project media, the university gate and the gallery. Education keeps its own character gate as its focal point. Reduced motion draws the complete static loop and disables flow, deformation and pointer response. React only owns the lifecycle; the renderer owns drawing. Detail adapts at 1440/768/390, device pixel ratio and update cadence are capped, and drawing pauses while the document is hidden. A local static glyph asset covers no-Canvas/no-JavaScript cases. No WebGL, Three.js, animation framework, analytics or network font is introduced.
