# Kallist portfolio

This portfolio connects inspectable engineering work with visual practice. Its language distinguishes a work's presentation from the evidence that supports it.

## Language

**Case study**:
A source-backed account of a project's problem, engineering decisions, delivered workflow and limits.
_Avoid_: Product advertisement, verified business outcome

**Work index**:
A concise, browsable overview of the engineering cases, giving each a distinct purpose and a direct route to its evidence.
_Avoid_: Skill ranking, popularity chart

**Screening room**:
A collection of user-supplied moving-image works with explicit context, attribution and intentional playback.
_Avoid_: Autoplay background, proof of model superiority

**Visual study**:
A supplied creative experiment whose presentation does not establish exclusive authorship, awards or a general model-quality result.
_Avoid_: Award-winning work, original film unless independently supported

**Evidence boundary**:
The distinction between what a source or executed validation establishes and what remains untested or unverified.
_Avoid_: Disclaimer, performance guarantee
